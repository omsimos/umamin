# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Umamin! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our open-source platform for sending and receiving encrypted anonymous messages.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

- **Authentication:** When you sign up or log in to Umamin, we collect your username and password. Passwords are hashed and securely stored.
- **Google OAuth:** If you choose to authenticate using Google OAuth, we collect basic profile information from your Google account, such as your email address and profile picture. We use this to avoid account loss, display your profile picture, and show your connected account's email in settings.

### 2.2 Usage Data

- **Google Analytics:** We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to collect information about how you use our platform. This includes:
  - Number of users
  - Session statistics
  - Browser and device information

### 2.3 Error Reports

- **PostHog:** When something breaks, we send an automatic error report to PostHog so we can fix it. A report contains the error message and technical stack trace, the page it happened on, and your browser and device type. If you were signed in, it also includes your account ID so we can tell whether a bug affects one account or many. We do not send the contents of your messages, posts, or notes.
- We use PostHog only for error reports. There is no session recording, no click tracking and no analytics profile.

### 2.4 Automated Content Checks

- When you publish a post, quote, comment or note, its text is checked by an automated classifier. We send the text, plus the post it replies to or quotes, through the Vercel AI Gateway to TypeSafe AI. We store the resulting scores with your content and use them to blur or fold it, to rank Hot, and to show possibly harmful content to our moderators for review.
- Private messages are checked only if the person receiving them turns on **Filter hurtful messages** in their settings. A message sent to anyone else is never sent for checking.
- Images are not checked. The classifier never deletes content or suspends an account on its own: people make those decisions.
- In the app, these checks appear as **Uma**. Uma is software, not a person.

### 2.5 Chats

- Chats are private conversations between two people who follow each other. We store each message encrypted on our servers. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted.
- Chat messages are not sent to the automated classifier.
- We keep a chat until one of you deletes your account. Deleting a chat removes it from your list only. The other person keeps their copy.
- If you report a message, we share it with our moderators along with up to 10 messages sent just before it. Moderators see who reported it. The person you report does not.

### 2.6 Advertising

- We show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads and measure them, including ads based on your earlier visits to Umamin and other websites.
- Where the law requires it, Google shows a consent message before it uses cookies for personalized ads.
- You can turn off personalized ads in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). Google explains how it uses data from sites that show its ads at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- We do not share your private messages or chats with advertisers.

### 2.7 IP Addresses

- We use your IP address to rate-limit requests and stop spam and abuse.
- If an IP address is used for abuse, our moderators can block it from sending messages, signing up or logging in. Blocking an IP address can also affect other people who share it.

### 2.8 Notifications

- If you turn on push notifications, we store the push address your browser gives us so we can send them. Turning notifications off removes it.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Authentication and Security:** To verify your identity and protect your account.
- **Service Improvement:** To analyze usage patterns and improve our platform’s functionality and user experience.
- **Advertising:** To show ads that keep Umamin free.

## 4. How We Protect Your Information

- We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. These measures include encryption, secure servers, and hashed passwords.

## 5. Sharing Your Information

- We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our platform, conducting our business, or serving our users, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.
- These are the services that process data for us:
  - **Cloudflare** hosts Umamin and stores uploaded images.
  - **Turso** hosts our database.
  - **Google** provides sign-in, analytics and ads.
  - **PostHog** receives error reports.
  - **Vercel AI Gateway** and **TypeSafe AI** run the automated content checks.

## 6. Your Choices

- You can choose not to provide certain information, although it may affect your ability to use some features of our platform.
- You can disable Google Analytics tracking through your browser settings or by using opt-out tools provided by Google. Browser tracker-blocking extensions also block our error reporting.
- You can turn off personalized ads in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can delete your account and all associated data in the settings.

## 7. Third-Party Links

- Our platform may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these other sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

## 8. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

- We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our platform. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
